Cue 02 · About

The person on the headset.

I'm an operations lead and stage manager in the MENA esports scene. My job is to make a show run on time, safely, and on budget: crews, vendors, run-of-show, crowd flow and the hundred small calls in between.

I've run everything from a 10-person campus booth to a 120-person arena production at Hassan Mostafa Sports Hall. Before FWZ I co-founded Atlas Coworking Space, which taught me the business side: budgets, suppliers and keeping customers happy.

Cue 04 · From the field

When the trucks don't show.

Load-in day

One hour to fix a logistics gap

Situation

On load-in day we were short of transport trucks, and the setup schedule was at risk.

Action

I went to the nearest transport stand, found out where the truck drivers gather, collected numbers and hired drivers on the spot.

Result

Extra trucks on site within one hour. Load-in stayed on schedule and the show opened on time.
